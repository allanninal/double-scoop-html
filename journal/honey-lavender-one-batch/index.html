Honey Lavender, one batch only

Some flavours are on the menu all year. Honey Lavender is not one of them. We make a single batch of it, we put it in the case at opening, and when it is gone, it is gone.

It starts with lavender steeped in warm custard until the whole kitchen smells like a garden. Then we swirl in Central Texas wildflower honey, which gives the gelato its golden colour and a floral sweetness that is hard to stop eating.

Check the Menu page or the case at any of our shops to see whether it is today's flavour. If it is, we would not wait until the evening.
